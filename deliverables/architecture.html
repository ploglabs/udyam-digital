Data flow through one journey

  1. Advise. The owner chats (or fills the short form). Gemini asks a few questions and must call recommend_bundle; the rules engine picks eligible products and prices, so the LLM can explain but never invent a price. A lead row (profile only) is created.
  2. Quote. /quote recomputes the monthly total, 18% GST, setup, contract and activation ETA live as add-ons are toggled; dependency errors block invalid carts.
  3. Consent. The DPDP notice lists purposes, data collected and not collected, and retention. Required purposes are recorded before any document can be uploaded.
  4. Smart KYC. Each upload is read by the vision model, the image is dropped, and only fields are kept. verify checks GSTIN format and check digit (with safe OCR repair), registry status and names, Udyam, signatory ID validity and bill age (≤ 90 days).
  5. Order and track. An order needs KYC that is not rejected and an active GSTIN for GST-only products. The tracker shows 5 stages with expected dates, replacing status-chase calls.
  6. Hand-off. Anything not closed (quote but no KYC, failed KYC, callback request) appears on the partner desk, ranked by propensity, with the next action.

Why it is built this way

  • LLM talks, rules decide. Prices, eligibility and KYC verdicts are deterministic and unit-tested; the form fallback works if the LLM is down.
  • Minimal data. No Aadhaar, PAN or bank details asked for; images never stored; identifiers masked in every response and log line.
  • Partners add, not compete. The digital flow handles the easy 80%; partners get a ranked, reasoned call list instead of cold leads, and can send the WhatsApp advisor link to leads who never had time for a visit.
  • Cheap to run. One small API, one database, pay-per-call LLM (4 calls per KYC). Scales out by moving SQLite to Postgres and the session store to Redis.
